# App Review notes

## Accounts

- Driver accounts are created by a company admin. They are not public sign-ups.
- In the driver app, account deletion is at Menu → Delete account. The app asks for confirmation before anything is deleted, then signs the driver out. The company admin is notified in the admin console.
- A driver with an open trip must end that trip before the account can be deleted.
- The passenger app’s public mode does not create an account, so it has no delete-account control.
- Passenger business mode (phone sign-in) has Delete account under Log out in Settings. Confirmation is required before deletion.
